## Outreach guardrails

- Use one tailored paragraph, one relevant guide link, and an optional 50-word blurb. Offer corrections. Do not infer acceptance or affiliation from an organization being listed on Baltimore.ai.
- Keep the action to reading, sharing, or registering through the organizer. Production email-dependent sign-in and claim flows are unavailable; do not promote listing claims or a Baltimore.ai newsletter.
- Recheck event pages and deadlines before sending time-sensitive copy. Proposed outreach dates are not commitments to attend, sponsor, or participate in events.
- No outside newsletter submission policy was found for Pava, UpSurge, TEDCO, or bwtech. A public contact route permits a relevant inquiry; it does not guarantee inclusion.
- AI Collective's event explicitly has **no pitch agenda**. Do not turn attendance into a product presentation. Its [code of conduct](https://www.aicollective.com/files/Code%20of%20Conduct%20~%20The%20AI%20Collective.pdf) also prohibits disruptive participation and permits removal for exploitative conduct. Ask the hosts whether a resource would help attendees.
- JHU's [poster call](https://ai.jhu.edu/fall-2026-symposium-call-for-posters/) is for research submissions selected for quality. Send promotional/resource inquiries to the institute contact, not the poster submission form. The current call confirms **Sept 16, 2026** as the submission deadline and **Oct 12** as the acceptance notification date. The [symposium page](https://ai.jhu.edu/event/data-science-and-ai-institute-fall-2026-symposium-artificial-intelligence-and-human-learning/) supplies official registration.
- Technical.ly's [current guidance](https://technical.ly/about/) favors a human story, a supported trend, and local relevance. Contact editors before submitting contributed drafts. Its [published pitch instructions](https://technical.ly/?p=48370) reject link-exchange/SEO pitches and allow a follow-up after one week. Do not promise or seek guaranteed editorial coverage.
- Do not use Techstars' accelerator application as a promotional submission or imply that accelerator participants are Baltimore-headquartered companies.
- TEDCO's portfolio promotional support does not establish Baltimore.ai's eligibility. Its sponsorship form is for qualifying event/opportunity sponsorship requests. The [marketing page](https://www.tedcomd.com/department/marketing-communications) requires approval for TEDCO brand elements; use text references and ordinary links instead of its logo.
- Do not present overall regional startup investment totals as AI-only funding. UpSurge's current Connect page says monthly; older pages describing a biweekly newsletter may be stale.
- The guide is not itself an event. Do not submit it to event calendars as one or submit another organizer's event under Baltimore.ai's identity. For example, [Visit Baltimore's calendar](https://baltimore.org/submit-event/) requires an actual event, a valid URL, a high-resolution image, and editorial approval.
- No bulk outreach, scraped private addresses, guessed email formats, reciprocal-link offers, or unsupported reach estimates. Log each actual send, response, and placement separately. Techstars, TEDCO, and bwtech remain planned for later outreach and have not been contacted.
