## Search record and stopping rationale

Discovery searched Baltimore AI companies/2026 developments, September community events, Johns Hopkins AI programs, and acquisitions affecting the prior directory. Two independent lanes audited the 30 seeded companies: healthcare/life sciences, and general/security/services. The coordinator covered institutions, events, statewide programs, ecosystem data and current official search guidance. Follow-up sought company contact pages, explicit AI product descriptions, completed acquisition announcements, organizer dates, regulatory/product distinctions and disconfirming geography evidence. A final bounded search filled the EcoMap/Pixee gap.

Search stopped when each included entry had supporting primary evidence and each consequential uncertainty was corrected, omitted or explicitly bounded. Further broad searches would mostly increase volume; unresolved physical-office or product-mechanism gaps remain grounds for exclusion rather than additional speculative copy. Independent workers reviewed the assembled company catalog, leading to source additions and the correction of CMS's update-date label.
