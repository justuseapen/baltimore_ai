The campaign asks readers to read, share, and follow organizer links. Production outbound email is unconfigured, so listing claims, magic-link sign-in, and newsletter signup are not conversion goals. Personal Gmail outreach is separate from application mail delivery.

## Search and AI discovery

Maintain crawlable HTML, unique titles, stable canonicals, visible citations, and accurate modification dates. Preserve the Google ownership tag in the shared layout. Google's [current optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) emphasizes useful original content and ordinary search eligibility, not hidden keywords or special AI files. [Sitemap submission](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap) is a discovery hint, not an indexing guarantee.

Check the live interface for available generative-AI settings and reports; repository metadata cannot verify them. Google's [2026 reporting announcement](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports) describes AI visibility reporting, but record only metrics actually exposed for this property.
