Baltimore’s AI directory spans healthcare technology, cybersecurity, research, and practical AI implementation. The useful distinction is **what an organization does and where it actually operates**. Baltimore City, Columbia, Catonsville, Owings Mills, and Fulton are different locations; a university affiliation alone does not make a company Baltimore-headquartered.

This guide was researched on September 8, 2026. The [company directory](/companies) contains source links and location notes for each included organization. It is a curated selection, not a census or a ranking.

## Which part of the scene fits your goal?

**Buying implementation help:** start with the [AI consulting directory](/categories/consulting). Describe a workflow, its data, and how you will measure a useful result. Ask who will maintain the system after the initial project.

**Exploring healthcare AI:** use the [healthcare guide](/guides/healthcare-ai-baltimore-hopkins) to distinguish data platforms, research tools, clinical devices, and accelerator participants. The approval or research status of one product does not apply to every product its maker offers.

**Working in cybersecurity:** the [Baltimore–DC corridor guide](/guides/cybersecurity-ai-baltimore-dc-corridor) separates AI used to support security operations from technology that protects AI or sensitive data.

**Meeting practitioners:** see the [fall events guide](/guides/baltimore-ai-events-fall-2026). University calendars and organizer listings are more reliable than assuming an old meetup is still running.

## Acquisitions and regional operations need labels

[Protenus](/companies/protenus) is a Baltimore-founded business acquired by Bluesight; [PGDx](/companies/personal-genome-diagnostics) is part of Labcorp. Their profiles preserve those histories without presenting them as independent startups. [ZeroFox](/companies/zerofox) became privately held after its 2024 acquisition. Sources: [Bluesight](https://bluesight.com/news/bluesight-expands-compliance-capabilities-and-strengthens-platform-through-acquisition-of-protenus/), [Labcorp](https://ir.labcorp.com/node/33121), and [ZeroFox](https://www.zerofox.com/press-release/haveli-completes-acquisition-of-zerofox/).

Leidos and Axle are labeled for documented local operations, not Baltimore headquarters. A current recruiting page can establish local work while remaining a fragile source for an evergreen street address. We disclose that distinction on their profiles.

## How this directory handles missing evidence

An entry needs a documented AI connection and a clearly described regional connection. We omit unsourced headcounts, guessed founding years, and unsupported product mechanisms. A hidden entry means we could not establish the required evidence; it does not mean a company has closed or does no AI work.
