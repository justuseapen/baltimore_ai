## Find the room that fits your goal

**Meeting practitioners:** the AI Collective event is presented as open networking for people building, using, or exploring AI. It is a first local networking night; we have not inferred a recurring monthly schedule from one listing.

**Discussing research and education:** the Hopkins symposium explicitly includes public attendance. Its schedule separates the daytime symposium, poster session, and evening debate. Confirm the session and access requirements on the university page.

**Building a health startup:** the Techstars deadline is an application opportunity, not a drop-in event. Review its selection process and program requirements before applying. The next scheduled cohort is in 2027, despite the application window opening in 2026.
