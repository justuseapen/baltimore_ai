**Johns Hopkins Applied Physics Laboratory is in Laurel, Maryland.** It is part of the region’s AI research landscape, but it is not the university’s Homewood campus, the Data Science and AI Institute, or a commercial startup accelerator. Start with the [APL laboratory overview and address](https://www.jhuapl.edu/).

## A concrete 2026 AI development

On April 13, 2026, APL [described in-house language-model training capabilities](https://www.jhuapl.edu/news/news-releases/260413-apl-in-house-llm). The work concerns building expertise in model development and adapting models to mission-relevant domains. It is research infrastructure and technical capability, not evidence that a consumer chatbot or independent company launched.

APL’s broader [AI research overview](https://www.jhuapl.edu/work/impact/artificial-intelligence) covers AI and autonomy across its missions. That page is a better guide to current work than an unsourced list of assumed company spinouts.

The [DSAI site](https://ai.jhu.edu/), [MINDS site](https://www.minds.jhu.edu/), and [Pava Center programs](https://pavacenter.jhu.edu/) describe their respective remits. Check the specific program and location before planning a visit.

## A university connection is not the same as an APL spinout

A company can have a founder who studied at Johns Hopkins, a research collaboration, licensed technology, an accelerator affiliation, or an origin inside a university lab. Those relationships are different. We use “spinout” only when a source supports that exact connection.

For example, [Sonavex](/companies/sonavex) identifies itself as a Johns Hopkins spinout in its [company announcement](https://www.sonavex.com/2025/04/28/sonavex-receives-fda-510k-clearance-for-echomap-device/). That does not establish an APL origin. The directory no longer treats every local AI company with a Hopkins connection as an APL spinout.

## How to follow the work

Use the laboratory’s own research and careers pages to understand the project, role, and access requirements. Do not assume every seminar is public or every position has the same eligibility. For an explicitly public gathering, consult the [fall events guide](/guides/baltimore-ai-events-fall-2026), which links to organizer registration information.
